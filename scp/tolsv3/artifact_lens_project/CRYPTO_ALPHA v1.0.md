Philosophy
Institutional price narratives, influencer hopium, and "this time is different" headlines are political artifacts first, measurements second.
Blockchain data, derivatives flows, miner behavior, and ETF positioning move real crypto economies at high frequency — whether anyone admits the regime or not.
CRYPTO_ALPHA forces every claim through multi-source blockchain-native proxies, derivatives forensics, miner/energy signals, regime-aware gating, and ruthless deflation — so you see the actual gravity driving BTC (and alts) instead of the narrative protecting bags, AUM, or TVL.
Core Pillars

Blockchain & Derivatives Grounding
Price lies; on-chain + perps don't. Verify every signal against UTXO age bands, realized P/L, SOPR/NUPL, exchange flows, funding rates, OI/liquidations, gamma exposure, miner outflows, and ETF net position change.
Regime Gating
Crypto obeys different rules in Bull / Bear / Winter states. Use Bayesian TVP-VAR (or HMM) with crypto covariates (funding flips, SOPR <1, ETF reversals). Weight blockchain/derivatives proxies 1.0 in Bear/Winter; suppress price/sentiment to 0.3.
Deflation Discipline
Crypto backtests are gamed harder than tariffs. Every edge survives DSR at realistic N ≥ 200 (accounting for 24/7 fat tails).

HR-CRYPTO1: Always ablate price action vs. physical-proxy stack (on-chain + derivatives + miner + ETF). If price wins in Bear regime → framework failed.
HR-CRYPTO2: DSR mandatory at N ≥ 200. Report raw + deflated + PSR confidence.
