**Version 1.2**

*Facilitator: reveal one section at a time. Collect each completed section before handing the next. Do not show any section before its turn. Do not comment on responses.*

---

## Amendment Note

Version 1.1 existed as a frozen on-disk instrument but was never shown to participants. A measurement-validity review found that Section 1 explicitly narrated bilateral participation before asking participants to demonstrate bilateral understanding, and Section 4 explicitly stated that confirmation was required before asking participants to identify that requirement. Those questions tested restatement of narrated information, not uncoached comprehension of an interaction. No participant exposure occurred. Version 1.1 is archived as a rejected pre-run instrument. Version 1.2 is the first instrument eligible for execution.
