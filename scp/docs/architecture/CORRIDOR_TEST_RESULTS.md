## Amendment Notes (Pre-Run)

**Version 1.1 — rejected pre-run instrument:**
- Version 1.1 was frozen as an on-disk instrument and never shown to any participant.
- A measurement-validity review found that Section 1 narrated bilateral participation before asking participants to demonstrate bilateral understanding, and Section 4 stated that confirmation was required before asking whether participants understood that requirement.
- These defects mean Version 1.1 could only prove restatement of narrated text, not uncoached interaction comprehension.
- Zero participant exposure occurred. Zero human-result contamination occurred.

**Version 1.2 — first executable instrument:**
- Section 1 replaced with two mock screens that do not narrate bilateral participation.
- Active moved to Section 2 in a post-establishment context.
- Warm and Dormant split into Sections 3A and 3B with separate page handoffs.
- Section 4 presents candidate UI copy and scores comprehension and emotional safety of that specific wording. A pass confirms the prompt is understood — it does not prove users would infer the recovery model from different wording.
- Section 5 retains the label hazard screen for Suspended, Severed, Burned.
- All fixed scoring rules preserved: 5 pairs / 10 individuals; Ambiguous does not count as Correct; Correct ≥ 8/10 required for pass; critical veto = any veto fails the term regardless of score count; blame/judgment framing = blocking design hazard.
