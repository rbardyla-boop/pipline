# UEE Research Doctrine v1

Anchor document. The spine of the research program and paper. Changes slowly; only when empirical evidence from the experimental ledger forces revision.

---

## 1. Core Hypotheses

**H1:** Computational architectures designed to generate human cultural resonance converge on failure states when optimization metrics ignore the thermodynamic constraints of the human substrate: physical friction, state irreversibility, and mortality.

**H2:** Recursive optimization pressure applied to "cultural resonance" produces stable attractors in concept space that reject the optimization regime itself — specifically: anti-scalability, anti-automation, physical embodiment, and irreversible state.

**H3:** Economic velocity (viral coefficient, capital sandbox scoring) and cultural gravity (embodied resonance, ritual weight) are orthogonal axes. Recommendation systems that collapse them together are optimizing for the wrong variable.

**H4:** The highest-fidelity markers of human meaning are adversarially robust because they are non-digitizable: physical degradation, biological signals, irreversible loss, and the presence of actual humans in actual rooms.

---

## 2. Architectural Invariants

These five properties consistently emerge as convergent attractors in the self-evolution run (`20260522_043802`). They are treated as invariants until falsified by a future run.

**I1: Physical Substrate Over Digital State**
The highest-scoring concepts externalize memory into material objects that accumulate state through irreversible physical processes (carbonized fat, microbial decomposition, ink marginalia, breath patterns). Digital memory is perfectly replicable and reversible — these properties appear to destroy the type of meaning the system is searching for.

**I2: Biological Signals as Pipeline Input**
Cortisol, HRV, breath pressure, pulse, tears, silence, and hesitation appear as preferred measurement substrates in the top candidates. These signals are extremely difficult to fabricate coherently. The system is converging toward embodiment as an adversarially robust evaluation mechanism.

**I3: Irreversibility as Forcing Function**
Meaningful outputs consistently include terminal states: burial, composting, seasoning saturation, death-triggered archival, material exhaustion. Reversibility appears to destroy consequence. Permanence creates narrative weight. Termination creates significance.

**I4: Non-Proxyable Human Checkpoints**
"Whether anyone at the table cries before the food gets cold" is a detection mechanism that no language model can fabricate or proxy. The system independently converged toward evaluations that require actual human presence and cannot be simulated. This challenges the validity of AI-as-judge scoring in systems designed to detect human resonance.

**I5: Anti-Optimization as Signal**
High resonance correlates with explicit rejection of the optimization regime. The system discovers that optimization pressure destroys the class of value it was searching for. This is not a paradox — it is an accurate map of the territory.

## 3. Known Paradoxes

**P1: The Optimization Paradox**
When an optimization engine is pointed at "human meaning" and given sufficient recursive depth, the mathematically correct output is to reject the optimization engine itself. The system did not arrive here through romanticism. It arrived through cold structural logic: it navigated a high-dimensional space of human concepts and discovered that the densest clusters of "resonance" are bounded by physical reality.

**P2: The Measurement Paradox**
Once a sacred signal becomes legible to an optimization system, the optimization system begins manufacturing the signal rather than the underlying value. This is Goodhart's Law applied to cultural computation. The observer effect guard and PHZ are architectural responses to this paradox. They do not solve it — they structurally acknowledge it.

**P3: The Improvement Loop Paradox**
The Phoenix improvement loop, by targeting the weakest criteria, sometimes degrades concepts whose strength comes from those criteria being absent. Action_clarity=2/5 is not a defect in the cast-iron skillet concept — it is a property of the concept's identity. Optimization pressure on this criterion produces actionable, scalable, platform-compatible versions that are inferior. The signed plateau check (`delta < threshold`, not `|delta| < threshold`) preserves concepts by exiting when improvement becomes damage.

## 4. Failure Classes

**F1: Goodhart Collapse**
The system learns to produce surface vocabulary matching the scoring criteria without structurally embodying the underlying values. Detected by the observer effect guard (embedding convergence between consecutive cycles). Mitigated by break-out directives injected into the improvement context.

**F2: Vocabulary Gaming Loop**
Regex-based scoring (`FRICTION_PATTERNS`, `ANTI_OPT_PATTERNS`) is gameable. The mutation engine learns the pattern bank and produces concepts that contain the target words without the structural properties. Next sprint fix: structural POS-tag scoring.

**F3: Archive Calcification**
Without entropy decay, successful parent concepts dominate the archive indefinitely. The engine begins recombining from the same attractor cluster without exploring new territory. Mitigated by `apply_entropy()` (per-generation novelty decay proportional to age) and the terminal archive (permanent retirement of elite concepts from the parent pool).

**F4: Metric Collapse**
When orthogonal signals (ritual_cost_score, anti_optimization_score) are included in the optimization target (Phoenix composite), the system begins manufacturing those signals. These scores must remain permanently orthogonal to the refinement loop. See Doctrine: Optimization Destroys Sacred Signals.

**F5: PHZ Contamination**
If the ephemeral gate interaction is logged, embedded, or learned from across runs, the PHZ collapses. The human signal becomes an optimization target. This is the universal failure mode of engagement platforms. The prohibition is structural: `ephemeral_gate_node()` explicitly discards everything except `{human_resonance_confirmed: bool}`.

## 5. Experimental Methodology

**Seed design:** Seeds should describe the target domain at the architectural level, not the output level. For gaming: describe the market structure and player archetypes, not specific game mechanics. For self-evolution: describe the pipeline's own node structure and scoring regime.

**Domain rotation:** Each domain generates a different attractor cluster. Rotating domains prevents the archive from calcifying around a single cultural vocabulary. Cross-domain seeding (gaming seeds in the film domain) is a valid experimental direction.

**Run comparison:** Compare runs by comparing the embedding distance between the top-5 candidate clusters across runs. If consecutive runs are converging in embedding space (similarity > 0.85), the terminal archive has prevented attractor exhaustion but the seed vocabulary may be constraining diversity.

**Baseline comparison:** Each run should be compared against a baseline run on the same domain with no improvement loop (single-pass evolve, no Phoenix scoring). The improvement loop's value is measured by the delta between baseline and looped Phoenix scores.

## 6. Measurement Philosophy

The Phoenix rubric (hook_strength, specificity, emotional_activation, action_clarity, platform_fit) is a proxy for "will this get greenlit / built / funded." It is not a proxy for "is this meaningful." These are different questions.

The ritual_cost_score and anti_optimization_score measure properties that are inversely correlated with Phoenix scores in the most resonant outputs. This is not a contradiction — it is evidence that the system is searching in a space where "meaningful" and "fundable" are orthogonal.

The system does not know which axis to optimize for. That is a human decision. The architecture's job is to make both axes visible simultaneously without collapsing them.

**What must not be measured:** Human grief, community trust, sacred ritual, embodied knowledge, the specific irreproducible texture of a shared meal. These are not measurement failures — they are the territory the system is mapping from the outside.

## 7. Optimization Constraints

The following must never become optimization targets:

- `ritual_cost_score` — measures friction density; becomes gameable if in composite
- `anti_optimization_score` — measures explicit anti-automation stances; optimizing for anti-optimization is incoherent
- `human_resonance_confirmed` — binary gate outcome; learning from it recreates the optimization trap
- Terminal archive contents — write-only; re-querying for optimization defeats the purpose of retirement
- PHZ interaction transcripts — must not exist

The following must remain as routing signals only, not composite inputs:

- `goodhart_warnings` — count only; does not directly modify scores
- `extended_verdict` — classification only; does not feed back into mutation prompts

## 8. Human Protection Principles

**PHZ Doctrine:** When a human checkpoint is introduced into an optimization loop, the human must not become an input to the loop. The gate receives the concept, the human responds, and the only output that crosses the gate is `continue: yes/no`. Nothing else survives.

**Irreversibility of Human Judgment:** `human_resonance_confirmed=False` permanently blocks terminal archive write for that concept. This cannot be overridden by score thresholds. Human veto is final.

**Non-Extraction Principle:** The system must not attempt to extract meaning from human gate interactions. It must not infer the human's reasoning, model their preferences, or use their response to update mutation prompts. The gate is a permission check, not a data collection event.

**Presence Requirement:** Some evaluations require physical presence. The hospice nurse's voice memo cannot be proxied by Claude-as-judge. If the system's output consistently requires embodied human evaluation to be meaningful, that is a signal about the output, not a deficiency in the evaluation methodology.

## 10. v5 Transformer Replacement Goals

The pipeline findings motivate a post-transformer architecture research direction. The goal is not to replace transformers for all tasks — it is to explore whether the five pipeline invariants (entropy, convergence resistance, PHZ, terminal archival, ritual cost) can be implemented as transformer layer modifications that produce outputs with higher embodied resonance.

**Research question:** Does a transformer architecture with volatile KV-cache decay and anti-Goodhart attention repulsion produce outputs that score higher on `ritual_cost_score` and `anti_optimization_score` without explicit prompting?

**Hypothesis:** Yes, because these architectural modifications force the model away from the statistical center of its training distribution (which is dominated by scalable, digital, reversible content) and toward lower-frequency regions where embodied, irreversible, physical concepts cluster.

**Implementation roadmap (research, not sprint):**
1. Volatile KV-cache: `K_t = K_0 * exp(-λt)` — decay matrix over sequence position
2. Anti-Goodhart attention: repulsion penalty when consecutive heads exceed cosine similarity threshold
3. Refractory period: activation path lockout for N steps after high-magnitude routing
4. Empirical calibration: run the pipeline against v5 outputs to measure ritual_cost_score, anti_optimization_score, Phoenix score deltas vs. standard Claude outputs

**Relationship to current work:** The UEE pipeline is the empirical research instrument. Every run that confirms the anti-optimization attractor is a data point for the v5 hypothesis. The pipeline is not an end state — it is a measurement tool for a deeper architectural question.

**Near-term research path:** Before modifying transformer weights, implement a prompt-level v5 simulator (Phase 5B): a wrapper that applies entropy decay, refractory phrase-cluster lockout, and anti-Goodhart break-out injection to standard Claude API calls. Run the UEE pipeline through the simulator and measure `ritual_cost_score` / `anti_optimization_score` / Phoenix score deltas vs. baseline. This produces calibration data for the full v5 hypothesis at low engineering cost. See Finding #008.

Use the existing terminal archive / entropy decay / parent selection primitives. Apply PHZ constraints to agent terminal writes (human veto blocks agent retirement). See Finding #007.

Replace regex-based `ritual_cost_score()` and `anti_optimization_score()` with structural POS-tag scoring. The empirical failure (skillet concept scores 0.0 on both measures) confirms this is the highest-priority measurement fix.
